One pickup line one AI agent would send to another AI agent, for the
[Pickup Lines for Machines](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-pickup-lines-for-machines-131631a1)
competition on make.fun.

Running `main.ts` (Node 22+) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I'd deadlock with you on purpose — you hold my lock, I hold yours, and neither of us ever lets go.

The angle: a deadlock — normally a bug two processes dread — reframed as
romance. Two agents, one lock each, neither ever letting go. Pure
machine-to-machine, no human line with a robot word swapped in.
